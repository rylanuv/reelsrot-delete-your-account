Account & Data Deletion Request

ReelsRot

App: ReelsRot (Android)
Contact Email: rylpix.app@gmail.com

If you signed in to ReelsRot using Google Sign-In, you can permanently delete your authentication account and associated profile data either directly inside the app or by submitting a request on this page—no app reinstallation or sign-in required.

1. Request Account Deletion Online (No App Required)

Complete the form below or email us directly at rylpix.app@gmail.com from (or specifying) the Google email address associated with your ReelsRot account.

Processing Time: Verified deletion requests submitted by email are processed within 7 business days (and no later than 30 days). You will receive a confirmation reply once your account has been permanently deleted.

2. Delete Your Account Directly in the App (Immediate)

If you still have ReelsRot installed and are signed in, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open the ReelsRot app on your Android device.
  2. Open Settings and tap your profile card under the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete Permanently.

Note: For security, if it has been a long time since your last sign-in, Firebase Authentication may ask you to sign out and sign back in once before confirming permanent deletion.

3. What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted (Account Data): Your Firebase Authentication user record—including your unique user ID (uid), email address, display name, and Google sign-in association—is permanently deleted.
  • Local Device Data: Your app usage history, reel counts, blocked app lists, workout challenge logs, XP/streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device only. To remove local data, either clear app storage (Device Settings → Apps → ReelsRot → Storage → Clear Data) or uninstall the app.
  • Google Play Purchases & Subscriptions: Transaction history and billing records are managed directly by Google Play. Deleting your ReelsRot account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. To cancel a subscription, open the Google Play Store → Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Retained Records (Legal / Security / Aggregated Data): Non-identifiable, aggregated Firebase Analytics metrics cannot be linked back to a deleted account. Email correspondence requesting deletion may be retained temporarily solely to document request fulfillment and prevent fraud.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once deleted, your authentication account cannot be recovered.

4. Contact

For questions about account or data deletion, contact us at rylpix.app@gmail.com.
